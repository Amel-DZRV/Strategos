Turn 3 · System director
Repositories are planets orbiting your machine. Click a planet to land on its surface map, where worktrees and Claude sessions are markers. Sessions started in the root folder sit at Local machine. Sessions that change several repos draw routes to each one (solid for edits, dashed for read-only) and appear as visitors on those surfaces. The sidebar filters by status and model.
